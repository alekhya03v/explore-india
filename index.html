Answers to common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to visit India?

India spans many climates. Northern plains are often more comfortable in cooler months, while hill regions and the southern coast have different seasonal patterns. Always check local conditions for your exact dates.

Can I plan a trip on a student budget?

Yes. Plan transport early, compare accommodation, use public transport when suitable and keep an emergency buffer. Our calculator is a rough planning tool, not live pricing.

Does the website save my personal trip plan?

Favourites, itineraries and the packing checklist are stored on your device using browser local storage. They are not sent to a server by this website.

Does Explore India provide travel bookings?

No. This independent educational website offers guides and planning tools only; it does not sell tickets, hotels or tours.
