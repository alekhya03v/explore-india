How to Plan Your First India Trip

Beginner India travel planning guide: choosing destinations, planning routes, checking the season and making a flexible itinerary.

Beginner India travel planning guide: choosing destinations, planning routes, checking the season and making a flexible itinerary. Use the following checklist as a starting point, then check current official guidance for your exact destination and travel dates.

1. Pick one region, not every region

India is enormous. Choose two or three places that are geographically close rather than racing across the country.

2. Consider the season

Check weather, daylight hours and local conditions; plans for coastal, desert and mountain regions can differ.

3. Build a flexible route

Allow realistic travel time and add a buffer day for a longer trip or difficult transport connections.

4. Check essentials

Confirm official entry requirements, transport schedules, accommodation availability and health guidance for your situation.

5. Save your plan

Use the itinerary builder on the homepage and export a copy before departure.

This guide is educational, not a real-time travel advisory. Check official information before making bookings or travel decisions.
