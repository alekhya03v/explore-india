Student Budget Travel in India

Practical student travel budget tips for India, with simple ways to plan transport, accommodation, food and a safety buffer.

Practical student travel budget tips for India, with simple ways to plan transport, accommodation, food and a safety buffer. Use the following checklist as a starting point, then check current official guidance for your exact destination and travel dates.

1. Set a daily allowance

Decide a personal daily spend for food, accommodation and local transport based on your preferred comfort level.

2. Compare before you book

Compare reputable transport and accommodation providers, and read current cancellation policies.

3. Keep travel days realistic

Overpacking a route can increase costs and reduce time available to enjoy each destination.

4. Prepare for extras

Include sightseeing fees, local transfers, taxes and emergency spending. Prices change, so verify before booking.

5. Use a simple estimate

Try the homepage trip calculator with your own assumptions and adjust the numbers as you research.

This guide is educational, not a real-time travel advisory. Check official information before making bookings or travel decisions.
