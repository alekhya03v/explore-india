About Explore India

Learn about Explore India, an independent student-created destination guide and trip-planning website.

Explore India is an educational travel guide built for a Digital Marketing practical assignment. It demonstrates semantic HTML, destination articles, on-page SEO, responsive design, accessible controls and browser-based planning tools. It is not affiliated with any government tourism department. The website does not sell travel products or collect trip plans on a server.
