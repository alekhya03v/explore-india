Munnar travel guide

Why visit Munnar?

Slow down among rolling tea-covered hills, cool air and misty viewpoints. Whether you prefer a relaxed visit or an energetic itinerary, allow time to explore at your own pace and be respectful of local customs.

Top things to see and do

  • Tea gardens — consider including this landmark or experience in your trip plan.
  • Eravikulam National Park — consider including this landmark or experience in your trip plan.
  • viewpoints — consider including this landmark or experience in your trip plan.

When to go

Commonly preferred travel months: September–March. Seasonal conditions, opening hours and accessibility vary. Check forecasts and official travel advisories near your visit.

Suggested two-day itinerary

  1. Day 1: Start with Tea gardens, explore local food and markets, and leave time for an evening stroll.
  2. Day 2: Visit Eravikulam National Park and viewpoints, adjusting your day around travel times and opening hours.

Practical travel tips

  • Check official opening times, transport options and entry rules before departure.
  • Carry drinking water, comfortable footwear and weather-appropriate clothing.
  • Respect photography restrictions, religious spaces and local communities.
  • Keep a flexible budget and allow for delays.
