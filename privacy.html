Privacy & Travel Disclaimer

Understand how Explore India stores saved places and trip plans, and the limits of its travel information.

This demonstration site stores favourites, trip plans and packing selections locally in your own browser (localStorage). Clearing browser data may erase them. It does not currently offer accounts or process bookings. Destination suggestions, dates and budget estimates are illustrative, not guarantees of availability, safety or price. Verify travel details with official sources before acting. If the site owner later activates analytics, this notice should be updated to accurately disclose any additional data collection and applicable consent requirements.
